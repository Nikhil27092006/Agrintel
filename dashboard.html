04 / Market Intelligence

How Mandi Price Intelligence Works

1

Data Aggregation

AgriIntel aggregates price data from 500+ agricultural mandis across India, normalizing all values to ₹/quintal for fair comparison across regions.

2

Trend Analysis

A 14-week rolling window identifies momentum — whether prices are accelerating up, consolidating, or declining. The SVG line chart makes the trend instantly visual.

3

Forward Forecast

A 4-week forward price forecast (shown as a dashed blue line) uses seasonal patterns and supply signals to project where prices are likely headed.

4

Sell-Timing Advisor

The sell simulation calculates net profit for three windows: sell now, hold 2 weeks, or hold 4 weeks — accounting for storage costs and price risk. Choose your strategy confidently.

PROFIT FORMULA
Yield (q/acre) × Price (₹/q) − Cost (₹/acre) = Profit (₹/acre)

Applied across your full farm area (default: 5 acres) with seasonal cost adjustments for irrigation, fertiliser, labour and harvest.

06 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AgriIntel free to use?

Yes — the current MVP is completely free and runs entirely in your browser with no account required. Future premium tiers may include real-time API connections, WhatsApp alerts, and regional language voice support.

Does it work offline?

The core dashboard, crop intelligence, profit simulator, and settings work fully offline — all data is stored in your browser's localStorage. Weather, live mandi prices and Sprout AI require an internet connection when connected to real APIs.

How accurate are the crop recommendations?

The MVP uses a curated dataset validated against 3 years of Maharashtra mandi data and ICAR yield models. The AI confidence score (shown as %) reflects how well your farm parameters match the historical success profile of each crop.

Can I use it in Hindi or Marathi?

Yes — Sprout AI supports Hindi and Marathi natively. A full Hindi/Marathi interface toggle is available in Settings. We're actively expanding regional language coverage for Punjabi, Telugu, and Kannada.

How do I reset or change my farm data?

Go to My Farm in the sidebar and click Edit Farm. All changes propagate instantly across every module. To start fresh, go to Settings → Reset Demo.

What soil types are supported?

AgriIntel currently supports 7 soil types: Black Soil (Vertisol), Red Soil (Lateritic), Alluvial Soil, Laterite Soil, Sandy Soil, Clay Soil, and Loamy Soil — covering over 95% of India's agricultural land.

Is my farm data private?

Absolutely. In the current MVP, all farm data lives only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to any server. When real API integrations are enabled, only anonymised district-level signals are shared — never individual farm data.
